Photoshoot Effect

In this tutorial, we are creating a photo shoot effect with our just-released PhotoShoot jQuery plug-in. With it you can convert a regular div on the page into a photo shooting stage simulating a camera-like feel.

Potential clients want to see who they’ll be working with. They want to hire people, not stock photography. We produced a custom photo shoot to bring the people front and center and to underscore the firm’s open and welcoming style.
